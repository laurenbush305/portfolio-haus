Opportunity Readiness Brief

For the moment when someone says: we have a proposal due and the story is everywhere.

Ten minutes of honest answers now saves the first hour of every meeting later. Nothing you enter leaves this page until you send it.

Working prospect tool · Used before deadline-driven work

Step 1 of 4

The Opportunity

Name the moment. Deadlines behave better once they are written down.

Give the opportunity a name. Even a working one.
This is the whole point of the brief. One or two sentences will do.

The Audience

Messages are approved by people, not by organizations.

Name the decision audience, even roughly.

The Message and the Proof

Claims travel. Receipts make people believe them when they arrive.

Risks and Success

Last step. Say the quiet part here so it does not surprise anyone later.

Describe the win, even briefly. It shapes the whole narrative.
