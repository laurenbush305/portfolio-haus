Work / A Few Receipts

What the work made possible.

Selected experience from Lauren Bush’s prior roles. These are examples of the judgment she brings to Portfolio Haus, with the delivery context credited in each case.

01 / Federal healthcare training

Help complex training work across people and settings.

Lauren’s role: Senior Director, Public Health & Behavior Change, The Hatcher Group. Delivered through The Hatcher Group.

The problem

A federal healthcare initiative needed recurring, high-quality training delivery, with coordination across educators, project managers, the client, and participants.

The work

Lauren supported TeamSTEPPS communications and training delivery, adapting complex information for different audiences and settings and coordinating the people involved.

The receipt

Nearly 500 healthcare professionals reached.

The work included training delivery and communication support. This is a team program reach figure, rather than a claim of individual delivery to every participant.

Why it matters

For healthcare and public health teams, clear language has to work inside a delivery system: with real participants, recurring schedules, and more than one person responsible for execution.

02 / Financial education

Make expertise usable enough to keep people engaged.

Lauren’s role: Advisory Board Member & Project Manager, Martin Investment Group, 2021–2023. Delivered through Martin Investment Group and Black Women Invest. Prior professional experience.

The problem

A 9,000-member financial education community needed curriculum, content, and engagement systems that could support learning and growth.

The work

Lauren designed curriculum, video content, and engagement strategies, alongside strategic counsel on program expansion and member-centered growth.

The receipt

40% higher user retention.
25% revenue growth.

Reported results associated with the curriculum, video content, and engagement strategies in Lauren’s professional record.

Why it matters

For education organizations and expert-led businesses, knowledge needs a usable learning structure. The output included curriculum and video content, supported by engagement strategies.

03 / Association communications

Give institutional work a clearer public presence.

Lauren’s role: Interim Director, Office of Access and Success, Association of Public and Land-grant Universities (APLU), 2016–2017. Prior staff role.

The problem

Access and success work inside a national association serving more than 200 universities needed stronger digital communications and audience engagement.

The work

Lauren implemented a new social media strategy for the Office of Access and Success, connecting content and audience priorities.

The receipt

133% more Twitter impressions.
72% more profile visits.
10% growth in new followers.

All three results were recorded within the first month of the new strategy.

Why it matters

Institutional communications can stay faithful to the work while making it easier to find and engage with. The strategic output was a social media approach grounded in the audience.
